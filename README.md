## English

Paste an image link, click on the car where you want the sticker, then resize, rotate and move it. The sticker is printed onto the car's paint and follows the shape of the body, like a real decal.

### Features
- 🖼️ **Any image**: paste a direct PNG or JPG link and the app downloads it.
- 🖱️ **Place with the mouse**: click on the car body to put the sticker there.
- 🔍 **Resize** with the mouse wheel or the size slider.
- 🔄 **Rotate and move** side to side, up and down, and forward and back.
- 🪞 **Mirror to the other side** of the car with one click.
- 🎯 **Any car** in the session.
- 💾 **Saves automatically**, so stickers come back every time you play.

### Requirements
- Assetto Corsa
- [Custom Shaders Patch (CSP)](https://acstuff.club/patch/)

### Installation
**Content Manager:** drag `FahadStickers.zip` into the Content Manager window and press **Install**.

**Manual:** extract `FahadStickers.zip` into your game folder:
```
Steam\steamapps\common\assettocorsa
```
The app should end up in `assettocorsa\apps\lua\FahadStickers`.

### How to use
1. In-game, open **Fahad Stickers** from the apps bar on the right side of the screen.
2. Paste a direct image link (PNG/JPG) and press **Add sticker**.
3. Click on the car where you want it. Use the mouse wheel to change the size, and right-click to cancel.
4. Fine-tune size, rotation, position and opacity with the sliders.

> **Note:** stickers are visible only to you. Other players online won't see them.
